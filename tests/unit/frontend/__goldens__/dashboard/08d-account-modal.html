Sign In to Sync Your Virtual Pantry & Preferences

Sign in to back up your Virtual Pantry, My Stores filter and Macro and Dietary profile across your phone, tablet and desktop.

By signing in, you agree to our Privacy Policy and Terms.

Minimal data. We store an opaque account ID, your email address and your grocery preferences. No passwords, street addresses or phone numbers.

Private by design. Every pantry entry and preference row is isolated to your account at the database (row-level security).

Yours to take or delete. Download a complete copy of your data, or delete your account and data, from Settings at any time.

Guest mode is always available. You can keep using PantryBot without an account for manual pantry entry and standard suggestions.
