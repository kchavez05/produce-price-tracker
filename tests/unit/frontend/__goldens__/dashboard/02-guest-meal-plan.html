Low-Effort Batch Cooking Steps:
  1. Cut pork shoulder into 2-inch chunks, toss with cumin, oregano, smoked paprika, salt, and garlic, and pressure cook on HIGH in the Instant Pot with 1 cup broth for 35 minutes (natural release 10 mins).
  2. While pork cooks, quickly char sliced cabbage, bell/poblano peppers, and onions in a wide skillet for 7 minutes until crisp-tender.
  3. Shred the pork and broil on a sheet pan for 4 minutes to crisp the edges; portion over the charred cabbage-pepper base.

Reheat & Storage Tip: Sautéed cabbage acts as a low-carb noodle/rice replacement that holds up for 5+ days in the fridge without getting soggy.
