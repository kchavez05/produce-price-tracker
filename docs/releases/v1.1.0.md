- **Release Date:** October 16, 2026

- **Production Environment:** [pantrybot.dev](https://pantrybot.dev)

---

## Executive Summary

PantryBot `v1.1.0` expands nationwide grocery coverage from the dual Kroger/Safeway banners to a **6-major-vendor ecosystem**, introducing **ALDI**, **Walmart**, **Target**, **Sprouts Farmers Market**, plus localized **Kroger** and **Albertsons** regional banners. A revamped **Universal Basket Optimizer** ranks single-stop shopping trips across all user-selected stores and computes the mathematically optimal 2-store split for maximum dollar savings.

---

## Key Capabilities & Highlights

1. **Multi-Vendor Grocery Integration (6 Major Supermarkets):**
   - **ALDI:** Deep-discount grocery staples with EDLP everyday low pricing, private-label produce, and no-coupon simplicity.
   - **Walmart:** Everyday Low Price (EDLP) and Rollbacks integration across grocery produce, bulk staples, and meat.
   - **Target:** Super Target & PFresh grocery coverage with Target Circle manufacturer deal tagging.
   - **Sprouts Farmers Market:** High-frequency organic produce, bulk bin staples, and specialty health foods.
   - **Kroger Regional Banners:** King Soopers, Ralphs, Fred Meyer, Fry's, Dillon's, Smith's, QFC, Harris Teeter.
   - **Albertsons Regional Banners:** Safeway, Albertsons, Vons, Jewel-Osco, Shaw's, Acme, Tom Thumb, Randalls.
2. **Store Registry & Banner Metadata (`src/data/store-registry.ts`):**
   - Centralized store configuration with brand hex accents, badge Tailwind stylings, loyalty app requirements, and regional zip-to-banner resolvers.
3. **"My Stores" Preference Drawer & Dynamic Filter Bar:**
   - Interactive store selector modal allowing shoppers to enable or disable any of the 6 stores based on local proximity and shopping preference, persisted in `localStorage`.
4. **Ranked 1-Stop Store Leaderboard & Best 2-Store Trip Optimizer:**
   - Universal N-Store Basket Engine calculating exact shopping list totals across all selected stores and evaluating all $\binom{N}{2}$ store pairs to identify the highest-savings 2-stop trip.
5. **Multi-App Digital Coupon Pre-Flight Checklist:**
   - Organized digital coupon clipping checklist partitioned by store app (Kroger, Safeway for U, Target Circle, Sprouts) with zero-friction EDLP tagging for ALDI and Walmart.
