# v1.5.2 — Data Clarity & Active Markets

The Data Explorer now explains seemingly repetitive source records and remains useful when local store history is sparse. Requested ZIPs can begin accumulating shared price history through bounded active-market collection.

## Explorer improvements

- Raw tables show package/source detail: original USDA price and size, meat package size, grade and condition, store advertisement conditions, and aggregation methods.
- USDA-only history displays explicitly labeled benchmark min/mean/median/max. Store weekly-best summaries and local patterns remain separate; missing data is not filled.
- Explain this Score shows a current benchmark or labeled static fallback and a hypothetical per-unit price calculator even with no retained offer. Optional regular-price inputs assert no discount when blank. The shared scoring formula and neutral six-point market contribution remain unchanged; hypothetical results never create observations.
- Sources & Coverage explains the editorial canonical ingredient catalog, included categories, exclusions, out-of-scope foods, and comparison limits. Category counts come from the catalog and a link preserves applicable filters into the detailed rules.

## Active markets

Dashboard requests register ZIP-only market interest and archive offers when they acquire a due collection claim. Initial limits are nine active markets, 30 days of activity after use, three claims per daily worker invocation, and a three-day refresh interval after success. Failed/empty collections become eligible after one hour. Fifteen-minute leases and token-fenced completion recover interrupted work; existing immutable offer revisions and retrieval receipts handle replay.

The authenticated `/api/cron/active-markets` worker requires server Supabase credentials and `CRON_SECRET`. Apply `20261029154017_active_market_collection.sql` before activation. The registry uses RLS, no browser grants, and service-role-only invoker functions. Coverage status is visible in the explorer. No-key operation remains available; admission or registry failures do not block the dashboard.

The Kroger connector's Mead-specific default is disabled outside ZIP 80542 unless an explicit location is supplied. Other markets depend on supported Flipp coverage until location discovery is expanded. Existing Wednesday/Friday 80542 syncs remain. Nationwide collection, store/ad-market deduplication, and provider-supported bulk access are roadmap goals; no historical local ads were recovered or fabricated for this patch.
