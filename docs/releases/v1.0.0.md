## Executive Summary

PantryBot `v1.0.0` represents the first production release of our automated grocery deal tracker and weekend meal-prep planning assistant. Built to solve the pain of high grocery bills and tedious meal planning, PantryBot cross-references live supermarket weekly circulars, digital loyalty coupons, and USDA wholesale commodity trends to construct low-cost, batch-cooked meals (stews, braises, and casseroles) averaging under \$1.50–\$1.95 per serving.

Initially developed and calibrated for Mead/Longmont, CO (`80542`), `v1.0.0` launches with **full nationwide dynamic ZIP code support**, allowing users anywhere in the United States to plan cost-effective weekend meal preps based on their local stores.

## Key Capabilities & Highlights

### 1. Nationwide Dynamic Location Engine
- **5-Digit ZIP Entry & Modal:** Users can switch their location anytime via the header pill.
- **Auto City/State Resolution:** Connects to `api.zippopotam.us` with in-memory caching and fallback to resolve neighborhood names instantly.
- **Cross-Banner Supermarket Scraping:** Connects to Flipp API querying regional banners under both Kroger (King Soopers, Kroger, Ralphs, Fred Meyer, Fry's) and Albertsons (Safeway, Albertsons, Vons, Jewel-Osco, Tom Thumb).
- **Session Retention:** Automatically saves preferred location to browser `localStorage`.

### 2. Affordability-First Value Scoring Algorithm
- **Formula:** 55% Affordability + 35% Discount vs. Regional Baseline + 10% USDA Wholesale Momentum.
- **Splurge Value Alerts:** Distinguishes everyday staple bargains from steep discounts on luxury proteins (e.g. ribeye roasts, wild sockeye salmon, and avocados at 40–60% off).
- **Redemption Clarity:** Clear badges distinguishing Store Loyalty Card prices, Clip Digital App Coupons, and Multi-Buy bundle requirements.

### 3. 3-Dish Weekend Meal-Prep Batch Planner
- **Target Profiles:** Stew, Braise/Chili, and Casserole/Curry designed for large-pot cooking and freezer stability.
- **Dynamic Portion Scaling:** Scales from 4 to 6 to 8 servings per dish, dynamically adjusting quantities and estimated nutritional macros.
- **Pantry & Freezer "$0 In-Stock" Offset:** Toggle ingredients you already have at home to drop their marginal out-of-pocket cost to \$0.00.
- **Gemini 2.5 Flash Generation:** Optional free-tier Gemini API integration allows users to generate bespoke recipes centered on any pinned circular ingredients.

### 4. Smart Shopping List & Coupon Pre-Flight
- **Shared Ingredient Deduplication:** Automatically consolidates aromatics, butter, onions, and root vegetables needed across multiple dishes.
- **Digital Coupon Pre-Flight Checklist:** Organizes digital coupons by supermarket banner with direct clipping reminders.
- **Store Split Optimizer:** Calculates whether splitting shopping across two stores saves enough money to justify the second stop.

### 5. Multi-Week Historical Pricing & SQLite Intelligence
- **Persistent Data Store:** Tracks rolling 6-week price histories using local/cloud SQLite (`data/mead-price-history.sqlite`).
- **Cycle Low Indicators:** Highlights deals hitting 6-week price cycle lows.

### 6. Mobile-First PWA & Automation Infrastructure
- **Progressive Web App:** Fully installable to iOS and Android home screens with custom icon, splash screen colors, and standalone display mode.
- **Weekly Automation:** Preconfigured Vercel Cron jobs at `/api/cron/weekly-sync` for Wednesday circular ingestion and Friday Weekend Digest alerts.
- **Hosting:** Deployed to Vercel production at `pantrybot.dev` and `www.pantrybot.dev`.
