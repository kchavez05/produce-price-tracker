## API and upgrade behavior

`/api/freezer` GET/POST/PATCH/DELETE return `410 Gone`, `code: freezer_retired`, and `replacement: /api/pantry` without reading or changing inventory. Keep these deprecated operations for at least one release after retirement.

`/api/sync` rejects the presence of `freezerItems` or `preferences.onHandIngredientIds` with `426 pantry_upgrade_required`, including empty/null values and unmigrated accounts. The update is not acknowledged. Reload PantryBot and explicitly review older inventory edits through Pantry. Owner-bound original queues and recovery copies remain available. New ordinary preferences can sync independently; later recovery cannot replay their superseded older values.

Original lots, manual selections, migration archives and data-rights export/delete remain intact. Historical saved recipe metadata never establishes current availability. No storage is dropped and no new hosted migration is required. Stage 3 storage deletion is a separate future approval and readiness/backup gate.
