## Executive Summary

PantryBot `v1.2.0` transforms the app from a single-week grocery circular tracker into a **multi-week smart kitchen inventory, nutrition personalization engine, wholesale club benchmark normalizer, and 1-tap curbside checkout bridge**.

Shoppers can now capitalize on deep 6-week protein cycle lows with `"❄️ Buy 2: Prep 1, Freeze 1"` arbitrage prompts, store extra roasts in a persistent **Virtual Freezer** (`SQLite` + `localStorage`) that automatically powers future 3-Dish Meal-Prep plans at **`$0.00` out-of-pocket protein cost**, customize **Macro Goals, Dietary Restrictions, and Cooking Vessels**, compare weekly grocery specials against **Costco & Sam's Club** bulk pricing, and bridge their deduplicated shopping list directly into **Walmart, Target, Kroger, Safeway, Sprouts, or Instacart** carts.

---

## Key Capabilities & Highlights

### 1. Persistent Virtual Freezer & Multi-Week Arbitrage Engine (Ticket #21 / `#513`)
- **Automated Arbitrage Prompts (`"❄️ Buy 2: Prep 1, Freeze 1"`):** Detects freezer-friendly proteins and produce hitting deep cycle lows (`>38%` off regular price, `💎 Splurge` alerts, or 6-week price floors) and computes the exact buy-now vs. freeze-for-later dollar savings.
- **Persistent SQLite + Client Sync (`/api/freezer`):** Added the `freezer_inventory` SQLite table with full `GET`, `POST`, `PATCH`, and `DELETE` endpoints synchronized with browser `localStorage`.
- **Interactive Virtual Freezer Drawer:** Manage banked poundage (`+/- 0.5 lb`), view purchase cost basis and cumulative savings, thaw/consume items, or manually log existing home freezer inventory.
- **Zero-Cost Recipe Substitution:** Any ingredient banked in the Virtual Freezer automatically merges into `onHandIngredientIds`, dropping its out-of-pocket recipe cost to **`$0.00`** and highlighting matching dishes with a `"❄️ Powered by Virtual Freezer ($0 Stock)"` badge.

### 2. Dietary Preferences, Macro Targets & Cooking Vessel Archetypes (Ticket #22 / `#514`)
- **Macro Goal Profiles (`MacroGoal`):**
  - `💪 High-Protein (40g+/serving)`
  - `🥑 Low-Carb / Keto` (automatically swaps high-carb potato/rice/pasta/bean slots to low-carb cauliflower, cabbage, broccoli, or green beans via `lowCarbCandidateIds`)
  - `🔥 Calorie-Conscious (<460 kcal/serving)`
  - `🍲 Balanced Family`
- **Dietary Rules (`DietaryConstraint`):** `🌾 Gluten-Free`, `🥛 Dairy-Free` (dynamically omits `isDairySlot` ingredients and adjusts macros/cost), `🥜 Nut-Free`, and `🐟 Pescatarian`.
- **Cooking Vessel Archetypes (`CookingVesselArchetype`):** Filter or boost recipes by `⚡ Slow Cooker / Instant Pot`, `🔥 Sheet-Pan / 9x13 Casserole`, `🍲 Dutch Oven Braise`, or `🍳 Big-Batch Skillet`.
- **Expanded Blueprint Library & Gemini 2.5 Flash Integration:** Expanded curated batch blueprints to 12 recipes and wired active nutrition/appliance profiles directly into `/api/ai-meal-plan`.

### 3. 1-Tap Curbside Cart Bridge & Multi-Store Export (Ticket #23 / `#515`)
- **Curbside Pickup Deep Links (`src/lib/cart-exporter.ts`):** Generates store-specific search URLs for **Walmart Grocery**, **Target Drive Up**, **localized Kroger banners** (King Soopers, Ralphs, Fred Meyer, Fry's), **localized Albertsons banners** (Safeway, Vons, Jewel-Osco), **Sprouts**, **ALDI**, and **Instacart**.
- **Per-Item & Store-Level Cart Launchers:** Launch all store searches from the Curbside Pickup Bar or click individual `"Add"` links on any shopping list row.
- **Apple Notes / Google Keep / Reminders Checklist Export:** One-tap clipboard export partitioned by store and aisle category (`Produce`, `Meat & Seafood`, `Dairy & Eggs`, `Pantry & Dry Goods`) with native `- [ ]` checkboxes.
- **Printable Kitchen & Fridge Slip:** High-contrast print modal and `@media print` CSS rules that strip navigation chrome for clean physical checklists.

### 4. "Stock Up vs. Wait" Predictive Cycle Low Signals (Ticket #24 / `#516`)
- **6-Week Historical Range Analytics:** Computes rolling 6-week minimum, maximum, and median unit prices in SQLite for every tracked commodity.
- **Traffic-Light Cycle Classification:**
  - `🟢 Stock Up Alert (6-Wk Low)` — At or within 5% of the 6-week price floor.
  - `🟡 Fair Sale Price` — Normal mid-cycle promotional range.
  - `🔴 Cycle Peak (Wait / Swap)` — Near the 6-week high or `<10%` off regular price.
- **Smart Substitution Recommendations:** Automatically attaches an in-category alternative currently on a `>25%` discount whenever an item is flagged as `🔴 Cycle Peak`.

### 5. Wholesale Club Price Normalizer — Costco & Sam's Club (Ticket #25 / `#517`)
- **29-Commodity Bulk Benchmark Dataset (`src/data/wholesale-club-benchmarks.ts`):** Normalizes Costco (Kirkland Signature) and Sam's Club (Member's Mark) bulk package prices into standard `$/lb` and `$/doz` units.
- **Real-Time Supermarket vs. Bulk Club Comparison:** Badges every deal card with `"🏆 Beats Costco/Sam's Bulk (-X%)"` when a supermarket loss-leader undercuts warehouse club bulk pricing, or `"📦 Club Bulk Cheaper"` when buying in bulk at Costco/Sam's Club is still lower per pound.
- **Expandable Club Comparison Matrix:** Shows exact Kirkland Signature and Member's Mark pack sizes and unit prices alongside the 6-week price trend chart.
