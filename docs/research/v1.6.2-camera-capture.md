# v1.6.2 camera capture integration (#647)

Implements approved BQ-04 under release tracker #645 and the unchanged [barcode decision record](../../catalog/proposals/2026-11-09-barcode-qualification/decision-record.json). Camera and public lookup remain disabled by default. This is implementation evidence, not physical-device or production acceptance.

## Flow and controls

`PANTRYBOT_BARCODE_CAMERA_ENABLED=true` additionally exposes **Scan barcode** inside the existing typed review. The pantry and barcode lookup controls must also be enabled. **Start / resume camera** deliberately imports the decoder and requests a rear-camera preference over a secure context. Opening the pantry or typed entry never requests permission or loads the decoder. The video is inline, muted and constrained to the container width.

Pinned `@zxing/browser` 0.2.1 and `@zxing/library` 0.23.0 decode locally with only UPC-A/EAN-13 enabled. Returned symbology, digit count, check digit and approved consumer scope are checked again. The first valid capture stops all tracks and decoder controls and fills the printed-digit field with its actual format. **Look up product** then reuses the existing bounded endpoint, correction and explicit **Confirm addition** flow. Capture itself sends no request and changes no inventory. Editing captured digits returns the declared format to manual validation.

Close, unmount, owner change, typed editing, lookup, hidden page and `pagehide` stop capture. Permission results arriving after cancellation stop their newly acquired stream; decoder controls arriving late are also stopped, and stale/repeated frames are ignored. Returning to the visible page does not restart the camera. Denial, absent media APIs, insecure context, unsupported decoded codes and decoder errors retain typed entry, catalog search and custom correction. No frames, device identifiers or error details are logged, uploaded or persisted.

The additional camera control leaves typed lookup available when disabled. Existing private owner-bound mutation/retry semantics, manual pantry entry and independent pricing controls remain unchanged. MIT and Apache 2.0 notices are distributed at `/licenses/zxing-browser-MIT.txt` and `/licenses/zxing-library-Apache-2.0.txt`, linked in the scanner.

Mocked media tests do not establish physical capture performance or mobile accessibility.

Real-device receipts are still required under #649 on **iPhone Safari** and **Android Chrome**, separately recording device/OS/browser versions, permission allow/deny, rear-camera focus, supported and unsupported codes, close/resume/hidden cleanup, typed fallback, narrow-screen layout, keyboard/screen reader, explicit confirmation and persistence. No real-device success is claimed here. Public activation additionally requires #648 artifact/deployment/shared-limit verification. #647 stays open until approved production activation and acceptance; #645 is not ready for release publication.
