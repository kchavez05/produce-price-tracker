# v1.6.1 market dairy review

Prepared November 7, 2026. **Proposed, not approved or active.** The accepted catalog remains `2026-11-07.2` with 199 ingredients. This packet covers the three remaining unmatched market groups: natural shredding/block cheeses, cottage cheese/sour cream, and Greek yogurt. It proposes **23 ingredients, 13 directional rules, 23 market associations, 16 scoped recipe decisions, one ordinary candidate removal, one required-slot split and 13 text edits**. Approval would allow implementation and validation; this document does not activate ingredients, prices, hosted IDs or recipe behavior.

The [review envelope](../../catalog/proposals/2026-11-07-market-dairy/review-envelope.json) hashes all nine exact payload files. The [36 curation hashes](../../catalog/proposals/2026-11-07-market-dairy/decision-hashes.json) cover 23 identities and 13 edges. Counterparts, slot decisions, the structural split and text edits also carry individual hashes. Review the exact names, aliases, source choices, offer policies and breakfast quantity allocation below.

## Choices to review

- Keep the common choice simple: plain cottage cheese, plain sour cream, and plain/vanilla Greek yogurt have unspecified-fat defaults; supported fat details are optional. A default can be recorded without selecting a fat level. Hot recipes separately require full-fat sour cream or whole-milk plain Greek yogurt; unspecified fat does not prove those requirements.
- Preserve fresh, low-moisture and moisture-unspecified mozzarella as distinct choices. Whole-milk or shredded wording alone does not prove moisture. Low-moisture whole-milk/part-skim choices are optional. None has a current recipe permission in this packet.
- Natural cheddar and plain Monterey Jack are separate from blends. Blocks and shreds share the same cooking identity; instructions explicitly shred blocks. Generic melting blends and named Mexican-style blends do not establish ownership of cheddar, Jack or any other component. Plain Jack excludes Pepper/Colby Jack; regular cheddar/Jack excludes explicit reduced-fat/fat-free and processed products.
- Keep vanilla Greek yogurt out of every savory scope. The existing ordinary plain whole-milk yogurt stays unchanged and never becomes Greek yogurt by inference. Greek discovery requires the word `yogurt`; fat-level choices stay behind optional precision. `milk` does not surface Greek yogurt.
- Split the breakfast casserole into **two required dairy needs: 8 oz plain cottage cheese AND 8 oz cheddar**. The old single slot treated them as alternatives. This explicitly reallocates its old combined 1.0 lb estimate into 0.5 lb each, rather than deriving portions from USDA. Existing macro estimates are retained and are not a new nutritional analysis.
- Remove cheese from the Tuscan soup finish instead of inventing an emulsion method. Retain full-fat sour cream or whole-milk plain Greek yogurt, added gradually off heat. Cottage cheese is allowed only in the blended casserole binder and the breakfast component; it is not an automatic sour-cream topper replacement.
- No new fixed prices, nutrition, global recipe links, dietary certifications or inventory mutations. Market association, availability and scoped recipe admission remain separate. Four earlier offer-qualification findings and non-market scope decisions still block final release sign-off.

## Exact recipe text edits (13)

The payload records complete before/after strings and hashes. These are the only proposed text changes; the required-slot split above is separate.

### `colorado-pork-green-chile-stew` — step 4

**Before:** Portion into meal-prep containers and top each with shredded cheese or a dollop of sour cream when reheating.

**After:** Shred block cheese before using. Portion into meal-prep containers; after reheating, top with natural cheddar, plain Monterey Jack or a Mexican-style cheese blend, or a dollop of plain sour cream.

### `cheesy-chicken-broccoli-potato-casserole` — slot label `creamy_binder`

**Before:** Sour Cream / Cottage Cheese / Greek Yogurt

**After:** Full-Fat Sour Cream, Blended Plain Cottage Cheese or Whole-Milk Plain Greek Yogurt

### `cheesy-chicken-broccoli-potato-casserole` — slot label `cheese_top`

**Before:** Shredded Cheese

**After:** Shredded Cheddar

### `cheesy-chicken-broccoli-potato-casserole` — step 2

**Before:** Whisk sour cream (or blended cottage cheese/Greek yogurt) with half the shredded cheese and ½ cup chicken broth.

**After:** Shred the cheddar if using a block. Blend plain wet-curd cottage cheese smooth if selected; otherwise use plain full-fat sour cream or plain whole-milk Greek yogurt. Whisk the selected binder with half the shredded cheddar and ½ cup chicken broth.

### `cheesy-chicken-broccoli-potato-casserole` — step 4

**Before:** Bake an additional 20–22 minutes until the selected florets are tender and cheese is bubbling golden brown.

**After:** Bake an additional 20–22 minutes, checking that the casserole and chicken reach at least 165°F in the center on a food thermometer and the selected florets are tender; continue cooking as needed.

### `southwest-sweet-potato-ground-beef-chili-bake` — step 4

**Before:** Stir in half the shredded cheese (or omit for dairy-free) and pack with fresh avocado slices for serving.

**After:** Shred block cheese before using. Stir in half the natural cheddar, plain Monterey Jack or Mexican-style cheese blend, reserving the rest for serving; if using plain sour cream instead, omit the cheese and add it as a dollop after reheating. Omit dairy for dairy-free and pack fresh avocado slices for serving.

### `tuscan-sausage-white-bean-spinach-potato-stew` — slot label `creamy_finish`

## Cooking evidence and limits

These are reviewed recipe choices rather than claims that every dairy replacement is equivalent. [MU Extension](https://extension.missouri.edu/publications/n689) describes higher curdling risk for lower-fat yogurt and cautions that substitutions are not always one-for-one. The [California Milk Advisory Board](https://www.realcaliforniamilk.com/baking/yogurt) recommends adding yogurt late with gradual heating and gentle stirring. Those support choosing whole-milk yogurt and explicit off-heat finishes, not granting universal permission to all fat levels.

[Arla Pro’s comparison](https://www.arlapro.com/en-gb/stories/why-does-mozzarella-make-pizza-soggy/) describes the different moisture release and handling of fresh versus low-moisture mozzarella; it supports separation, not recipe validation for this app. [FoodSafety.gov](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures) supports the 165°F chicken/casserole thermometer endpoint added during the binder edit.
