# v1.6.1 final catalog audit

Audited November 6, 2026 against catalog `2026-11-06.4` (145 identities). **Audit complete; release work required.** This review changes no active catalog, inventory, prices, recipe rules or hosted environment. The maintainer directed us to **close market coverage gaps before release**; 145 is therefore the current count, not a fixed release target.

## Findings and decisions

All 42 original research choices are approved and integrated (44 identities because coriander and cardamom each have separate whole/ground forms). The 22 approved scoped recipe uses remain intact. All 145 ingredient names and 51 aliases are reachable; all 170 current search fixtures pass. Broad queries remain within 12 results, including beans at 12 and flour at ten. Exact No, custom-entry isolation and optional-variety availability boundaries pass.

Keyword extras matter: leg quarters, tenderloin, ribeye/T-bone, coho salmon, red potatoes, pasilla/Hatch/serrano, kabocha, Cosmic Crisp/Granny Smith and Mexican cheese blends cannot be hidden behind a broad group label. Some lack exact retained source evidence; others have records for a different form (for example dried pasilla). Generic defaults and optional precision need explicit review before the final addition count is known.

Counterpart associations must be represented separately from recipe compatibility. Owning one member never establishes every food in its market group. Review each recipe slot and original offer title before assigning a price: jalapeños do not automatically meet a bulk poblano slot; spaghetti squash does not automatically meet a cubed butternut requirement; steak coverage does not require inventing a recipe use. Preserve market history and reject unsupported or ambiguous offers rather than relabeling them.

Four existing qualification findings also need resolution: generic avocado keywords do not prove Hass; fresh chicken breast does not prove boneless/skinless; generic cabbage does not prove green; retail “yams” does not establish true-yam/sweet-potato equivalence. These are identity/offer-admission questions, not permission to attach more sources.

## Reproduction and release gates

Run from the repository root with the retained full review snapshot:

```sh
node --import tsx scripts/audit-cooking-catalog.ts \
  --review data/fdc-catalog-review/2026-10-30-fingerprinted \
  --output catalog/audits/2026-11-06-final \
  --as-of 2026-11-06 --check
```

Omit `--check` and select a **new** output directory to generate a separate report. The tool refuses to overwrite reports or write into source/proposal/runtime inputs. The ignored full snapshot is required; the 40-row evidence subset cannot replay all prior approvals. `run-envelope.json` contains the execution timestamp and is excluded from deterministic comparison.

Validation covers active approval/source replay, all inherited approvals, unchanged runtime and compatibility, 58 gap assessments, source eligibility, proposed payload hashes, both discovery sets, 42-choice closure, 74-row hold, 18-group coverage plan, additive registry migrations and retained rollback views. The [rollout report](../../catalog/audits/2026-11-06-final/rollout-readiness.json) checks five expansion migrations covering 73 new IDs since the accepted 72-identity baseline and retained 72/101/111/134/138 views. It does **not** verify a hosted environment. Market expansion will require further additive registry migration(s).

Before release: approve and integrate exact provenance decisions and scope choices; close required market gaps through reviewed payloads and implementation; refresh the final audit against that artifact; verify hosted accepted IDs and account/guest persistence; complete version/release documentation and the required develop → main review/deployment workflow. Preparing this audit does not release v1.6.1.
