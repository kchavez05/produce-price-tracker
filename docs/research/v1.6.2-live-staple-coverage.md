## What the source establishes and leaves open

- Prices exist in Production, rather than only in Certification. Same-day retrieval establishes observation time, not that a listing is unconditional, current stock is guaranteed, or its product record was updated that day.
- `items[].price.regular` and `promo` are distinct. Preserve both as evidence and exclude `promo` from the approved initial policy. The sampled response supplies no explicit currency or complete purchase-condition object; verify provider semantics before admitting a regular field as an unconditional USD shelf quote. Empty restrictions alone is not proof.
- Broth representatives expose bare `32 oz`, and Worcestershire exposes `fo`. Do not reinterpret those strings as validated net volume or derive package size from nutrition serving counts. Confirm label evidence or a reviewed source-specific unit contract first.
- Oils, vinegar and ready-to-use juices provide explicit fluid-ounce examples; lemons provide `1 each`. Cooking sprays, oil blends, juice drinks, lemon juice returned for a lime search, concentrates and multipacks must not bypass food/form or package parsing.
- Canned-bean mass and canned-tomato mass are potential same-basis candidates, but canned form and recipe net-content meaning still need review. Do not infer drained yields. Premium coconut milk alone does not prove the full-fat canned requirement of the current curry.
- Retain the prior 26 static-only identities and all reviewed static fallbacks. No density conversion, new static value, catalog identity, pantry mapping or nutrition import is added.
- Remaining decisions: provider usage/retention terms, source currency/conditions contract, unit vocabulary and cooking-volume convention, recipe/form admission, deterministic selected-store offer/tie policy, caching/freshness/rollout controls and shared recipe/shopping implementation. Production access is resolved; those gates are not.
