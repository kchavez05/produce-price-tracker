## Identity and search boundaries

- **Oils and ground spices:** sesame oil is unspecified cooking sesame oil; the generic source does not establish toasted or untoasted identity. Ground cinnamon/cloves/turmeric/ginger remain distinct from whole spices and fresh roots. Ground ginger never establishes the existing fresh Ginger entry.
- **Miso and sauces:** Miso is proposed as the ordinary cooking-paste identity with variety unspecified. Dry soup mix, constituent soybeans/rice, fish inventory/stock, and mushroom-based oyster-sauce alternatives are not aliases or derived coverage. No dietary guarantee follows from a generic condiment identity.
- **Tofu and tempeh:** soft texture stays separate from existing Firm tofu. Source coagulants do not create calcium claims. Plain tempeh does not establish a flavored product, cooked dish, or named legume composition.
- **Milk and yogurt:** Whole milk retains the named 3.25% description, without fortification/nutrition guarantees or plant/skim/reduced-fat equivalence. Yogurt is explicitly plain whole-milk, not every fat level, Greek yogurt, or vanilla yogurt. `milk` search returns milk choices without yogurt; `yogurt`, `plain yogurt`, and `plain whole milk yogurt` reveal the precise whole-milk choice. A query lacking `yogurt` does not reveal that form-qualified entry.
- **Cheese:** Feta is not limited to a crumbled package and makes no milk-species or origin certification claim. Hard Parmesan does not establish an unspecified grated product or protected-origin certification. Cheese aliases retain these distinctions.
- **Tuna:** light tuna canned in water remains separate from oil-packed tuna; drainage is source context, not a new default choice or portion conversion.
- **Nuts and seeds:** whole raw almonds remain separate from blanched/roasted nuts, flour and butter. English walnut halves are source shape context, not a requirement that the user own a particular package shape; black/roasted/glazed walnuts are distinct. Whole dried sesame seeds do not establish hulled/toasted seeds, oil, or tahini.

All 23 additions remain unpriced. No fixed estimate, market price, nutrition import, recipe requirement, dietary guarantee, substitution, automatic inventory change, or new coverage edge is introduced. Optional quantities and dates do not change explicit availability. Existing ingredient fields, prices, recipe links, and compatibility remain intact.
