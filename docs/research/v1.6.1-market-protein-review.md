## Main choices and boundaries

- Raw versus fully cooked sausage is a material distinction. Include both for smoked sausage, kielbasa and andouille; a smoked/style/brand label alone proves neither state. Raw pork sausage styles remain distinct from unknown-species raw smoked/kielbasa/andouille products.
- Raw protein identities include chilled or frozen unprepared products of the stated food/cut. These recipes require thawing first. Freezing is storage context for these protein choices, not a raw-to-cooked conversion or a change to the approved fresh-produce policy. Frozen prepared/breaded/marinated products remain excluded.
- Keep bone-in thighs, drumsticks and quarters separate from the existing boneless skinless thigh identity. No source-based retail-bone or edible-yield conversion is proposed.
- Boneless loin roast and chops remain separate from tenderloin. Keep named steak cuts distinct. Ribeye, strip, sirloin, generic pot-roast/stew beef and arm roast make no unstated retail bone/trim promise; the explicitly boneless chuck and loin identities require that qualification.
- Salmon has one generic raw-fillet default with optional Atlantic, sockeye and coho precision. No generic ownership implies a species or farming method. Fillet identity is a reviewed edible-fish cooking choice; analytical source flesh does not establish retail skin/bone trim or whole-fish yield.
- All new entries remain without fixed prices and global `recipeIngredientId` links. Market association is separate from ownership and recipe permission. Legitimate foods can have market coverage without fitting a current recipe.

## Three separately reviewed coverage rules

Atlantic, sockeye and coho raw fillets may each cover the generic `salmon-fillets-raw` requirement through one-hop same-family/same-form edges. Generic salmon No overrides derived species coverage. Generic salmon Yes never establishes a named species, and one species never establishes a sibling. There are no other new compatibility edges: raw does not establish fully cooked; bone-in does not establish boneless; tenderloin does not establish loin roast; steak or generic stew beef does not establish boneless chuck.
