The approved first batch adds evidence to six inherited entries without changing their prices or recipe behavior. The compiled catalog now has 101 identities and 39 source links across 38 identities.

## Market and identity blockers

- Split or explicitly model alternatives for broccoli/cauliflower, spinach/kale, white/cremini mushrooms, and tomatoes/tomatillos before publishing ingredient-specific recipe links. Existing group names and offers are not evidence of ownership of every member.
- Ordinary plain yogurt is not the existing market group for plain/vanilla Greek yogurt. Review cottage cheese/sour cream and other composite groups when reaching those forms.
- The current market catalog has no grain/flour/legume counterpart for the first batch. Do not invent prices from FDC data or link different forms.
- Three otherwise valid cooking ingredients—granulated sugar, brown sugar, and honey—are excluded by current discovery heuristics. A future batch can record explicit inclusion rationale; do not silently change importer rules or treat the excluded rows as automatically approved.
- Further beans need a discovery/grouping decision: the first proposed batch already fills the broad `beans` result limit. Optional lentil varieties need species/form evidence rather than relabeling generic dried lentils.
- Fresh/frozen/canned vegetables, whole/ground spices, toasted/untoasted oils/seeds, yogurt types, and tofu texture require explicit granularity decisions. The shortlist does not claim those categories are complete.

## All 74 quarantined Foundation records

The existing hold is unchanged: source release 2026-10-29, Catalog maintainer ownership, review due 2027-04-30. No held row is linked or cleared. Comparing normalized descriptions against **candidate-only** Foundation/SR rows yields:

- 66 held rows with at least one Foundation candidate-description match.
- Four additional held rows with SR-only matches: frozen carrots, navel oranges, Bartlett pears, and whole-wheat bread.
- Four with no candidate-description match: smooth salted peanut butter, commercial white bread, granulated sugar, and restaurant sweet-and-sour pork.

These are investigation hints, not confirmed replacements. This comparison uses candidate-only matches across both source types and is distinct from the earlier initial-review count of 68 description hints. For example, granulated sugar has an excluded candidate source, which this candidate-only tally intentionally does not count. Review subtype membership, source fields/fingerprints, cooking relevance, and form before any replacement decision. NDB equality is recorded per hint but never establishes equivalence.

A likely disposition for a finished restaurant dish is exclusion, while pantry cooking ingredients may need a subtype-backed source or a documented curated gap. These remain proposals to investigate, not recorded final decisions.
