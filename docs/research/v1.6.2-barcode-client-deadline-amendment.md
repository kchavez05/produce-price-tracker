## Amended contract and evidence

The browser aborts a barcode lookup after **2,500ms**, replacing its prior 2,000ms timer. The timer covers receiving response headers and reading the complete response body, so headers arriving before the deadline do not exempt a stalled body. This is a bounded client wait, not a guarantee that every request succeeds within it. A timeout remains a recoverable service failure with manual selection/retry; it never becomes a confirmed missing product or inventory write. One client lookup remains active at a time, with cancellation and stale-result/owner guards unchanged.

The approved request-body read timeout remains two seconds, the input cap remains 256 bytes, and the function ceiling remains five seconds. Complete artifact SHA-256 verification, read-only access, source/mapping coverage, response-size limits, the [regional 30/IP/60-second control](v1.6.2-barcode-regional-limit-amendment.md) and throttling recovery remain unchanged. No additional automatic retry is introduced.

The [losslessly compacted artifact](v1.6.2-barcode-compaction.md) preserved all logical data while reducing size by 47.3%. Its protected Preview observed a first lookup at 1024ms, warm lookups at 216ms, and a parallel maximum of 2039ms: one response exceeded the former two-second timer. These bounded observations support the budget decision, not a guaranteed cold-start or production/device result. Fresh qualification must verify the implementation against the amended contract and retain any observed failures.

Tests exercise a response arriving at 2200ms, successful timer cleanup, a stalled request remaining active at 2499ms and aborting at 2500ms, plus manual selection after timeout without a write. Hosted qualification remains separate from these simulated transport tests.
