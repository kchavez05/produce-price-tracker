# v1.6.1 final qualification and release scope review

Prepared November 7, 2026. **Proposed, not approved or active.** This packet resolves the four outstanding offer-qualification findings and presents the five remaining non-market release boundaries for explicit review. The accepted catalog remains `2026-11-07.3`: 222 ingredients, 252 ingredient decisions, 81 independent counterparts, 63 scoped uses and 20 protected mixed groups. The proposed changes add no ingredient, source, alias, compatibility edge, fixed price or accepted registry ID. No database migration is required.

The [review envelope](../../catalog/proposals/2026-11-07-final-qualification/review-envelope.json) hashes eight exact payload files. It binds four offer rules, four counterpart records, all 18 affected recipe candidate decisions, three removals/exclusions, 11 text edits, five scope boundaries, 71 offer expectations and eight existing availability examples. The active runtime, blueprints, source/curation/coverage ledgers and historical artifacts are unchanged at this research stage.

## Decisions to review

- Require actual Hass evidence for Hass avocado, actual green-cabbage evidence for green cabbage, and raw **boneless AND skinless** chicken-breast evidence for that existing chicken choice. A brand, generic food name, size or package description supplies none of the missing qualifiers.
- Require explicit sweet-potato evidence on the actual offer. A one-food label such as “Garnet yams (sweet potatoes)” qualifies because it names sweet potato. Bare “yams,” “Garnet yams,” true yam, Dioscorea and ube do not establish the existing sweet-potato identity. No new yam alias or botanical conversion is proposed.
- Apply those rules before current market/explorer scoring and per-store selection, then recheck direct/cached recipe repricing and shopping. Preserve observed rows, historical group assignments, canonical market names/keywords, retail units and selected-store pools. Unqualified offers remain observed history rather than verified current food-specific quotes. Group benchmarks without exact food evidence cannot establish qualified-identity savings; retain that uncertainty or mark the comparison unavailable.
- Keep all four existing precise pantry IDs and their approved exact recipe links. They are not mixed food groups and must not become globally protected or lose exact ownership in legitimate/custom recipes. A stored Hass Yes still means Hass; an exact No still overrides that exact identity.
- Remove green cabbage from the cauliflower-mash slot, implementing the previously approved cauliflower-only direction. Also remove green cabbage from Colorado stew’s cauliflower-only low-carb method and sweet potato from the Dutch-oven potato slot lacking a reviewed sweet-potato braise method. Retain the other 15 exact candidates with the explicit preparation text below.
- Block stale matched slots **and literal saved requirements** for those three excluded foods under the three stable built-in recipe IDs. Each excluded food has no other legitimate remaining slot in its named recipe. This narrow exclusion must not globally block green cabbage or sweet potato, alter stored inventory, or remove their other recipe uses.
- Retain v1.6.1’s current reviewed category boundaries and defer the five explicitly listed non-market expansions. This is a release scope decision, not a claim that all culinary ingredients are complete or a permanent exclusion. All currently approved market members, source holds, qualification findings and hosted/release gates remain required.
