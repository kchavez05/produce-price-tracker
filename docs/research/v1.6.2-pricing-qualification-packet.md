## 1. Units and narrow overrides

Propose US customary measures for existing unqualified recipe `cup`, `tbsp` and `tsp`. [Unicode CLDR's conversion table](https://www.unicode.org/cldr/charts/49/supplemental/unit_conversions.html) distinguishes customary, metric and imperial systems. Use these exact decimal factors, represented as rationals rather than rounded intermediate floating-point amounts:

| Unit | Exact base amount |
| --- | ---: |
| US fluid ounce | 29.5735295625 mL |
| US cup | 236.5882365 mL |
| US tablespoon | 14.78676478125 mL |
| US teaspoon | 4.92892159375 mL |
| mass ounce | 28.349523125 g |
| pound | 453.59237 g |
| liter | 1000 mL |
| kilogram | 1000 g |

Accept positive finite decimal amounts with a declared compatible unit and bounded precision. Keep original source labels. Initial runtime parsing should accept single-package explicit `fl oz`/`fl. oz`, mL/L and supported mass units, plus exact `each`/count evidence for the reviewed lemon; reject ambiguous multipacks. Do not treat a bare `oz` as volume, or a fluid ounce as mass. Interpret bare `oz` as mass only with reviewed net-mass evidence. Recipe `each` requires actual count evidence, never an average weight. Density, drained yield, concentration, portion-count multiplication and inferred clove/leaf weights stay excluded. Conflicting labels fail qualification.

Nutrition-label rounded measures such as a 240 mL cup or 5 mL teaspoon do not redefine the cooking convention. Do not multiply serving size by servings per package to create a net package amount. Metric-versus-customary display units must remain identified.

**Exact Worcestershire exception proposed:** Kroger Production product `0005160000004`, source size `15 fo`, may use 15 US fluid ounces only while bound to the reviewed US Original Lea & Perrins product/size and corroborating source revision. The [manufacturer's exact variant page](https://www.kraftheinz.com/lea-perrins/products/00051600000044-the-original-worcestershire-sauce) identifies a 15-fluid-ounce bottle and a gluten-free claim, alongside anchovy ingredients. Its trade-item reference is `00051600000044`; record the explicit provider-to-manufacturer binding, not a general barcode transformation. A different size, formulation, country, source revision or contradictory label requires review. Manufacturer gluten-free evidence can qualify the existing gluten-free sauce identity; it adds no general dietary certification or pantry compatibility edge. Never infer component ownership from the ingredient statement.

Hold chicken and beef broth measured in cups: current source `32 oz` is insufficient. The [foodservice chicken-broth page](https://www.campbellsfoodservice.com/product/chicken-broth-6/) is a different trade item and does not establish a retail override. A matching retail label or provider contract must establish net volume first. No global `fo` alias is proposed.

## 3. Quote admission, freshness and selection

[Kroger's Products reference, version 1.3.0](https://developer.kroger.com/api-products/api/product-api-public), inspected November 9, distinguishes local and national prices, regular and promo amounts, and estimated per-unit fields. Its store-sale flag does not guarantee stock; inventory is separate. The public Products allowance is 10,000 calls/day, and term search is fuzzy. The reference does **not** establish unconditional USD shelf semantics from the bare regular field. These source facts replace the previous documentation-access gap.

Proposed required quote fields: provider/environment; stable product/item and revision evidence; exact ingredient/form admission version; verified market and location; original size and parsed positive net amount/unit/basis; integer package cents; currency and purchase-condition evidence reference; observation/effective/expiry times; store-sale and inventory status; attribution/retention permission; and a traceable rejection reason. Source records are not personal pantry records.

Use only `items[].price.regular` from a Production request explicitly bound to the selected location. Reject missing/unknown currency or conditions, certification data, source conflicts, nationalPrice, promo-only amounts and both per-unit-estimate fields. A successful HTTP response, empty restrictions or a US store address alone establishes neither unconditional eligibility nor the currency contract. Once documented at provider/endpoint/market level, those semantics may be referenced by version; a currency need not be fabricated into the original payload. A genuine free offer requires explicit free-price evidence; missing price is never zero.

Require a non-future observation on the same America/Denver calendar day, started source validity and no expired applicable timestamp. Date-only validity uses the documented source timezone; unknown timezone blocks admission. Effective/expiry fields whose regular-versus-promo meaning is unresolved cannot be used to extend regular-price validity. Apply a conservative earlier expiry until the source contract is settled. Reevaluate on serving, day, market, selected locations, source revision or availability changes.

Require sold-at-location evidence. Reject `TEMPORARILY_OUT_OF_STOCK`; HIGH/LOW may remain eligible, and missing inventory may remain eligible with **Stock unconfirmed**. Never label `inStore=true` as guaranteed stock. No inventory condition can override unknown source currency/price terms.

After deduplicating revisions and rejecting unresolved conflicts, compare eligible offers using exact same-basis allocated unit cost, without changing food/form or pinned alternatives. Break equal-cost ties by newest observation, then location ID, product ID and item ID lexicographically ascending. Compare only selected locations in the approved market. Use the same chosen offer everywhere for that price context. The result minimizes allocated cost, not checkout expense. Prefer product-details requests with explicit location binding; do not rely on product-search parameter combinations documented to ignore other filters.

## 4. Calculation and presentation contract

Propose integer package cents and exact bounded rational amounts. Source money must be exactly representable in supported cents or rejected; do not silently round malformed source prices. Validate every quantity, conversion, product, denominator and aggregate range. Use an exact internal representation with checked output bounds rather than overflowing floating-point math.

`allocated cents = package cents × compatible required amount / compatible net package amount`

At a single-recipe evaluation, retain exact staple costs until aggregation. Combine them with the existing authoritative market-slot cents, round the recipe total once half-up, and allocate staple display cents using largest fractional remainders with a stable requirement key for ties. Keep duplicate requirements distinct by stable recipe/slot key during reconciliation. Detail rows must sum to the displayed recipe total. Combined shopping sums the authoritative allocated rows for included recipes rather than independently repricing and rerounding them. Aggregating quantities for display must preserve their allocated cents and provenance. Detached shopping needs use their own explicit evaluation scope.

The shared context binds observation data/rules, market/location selection, evaluation time/day, servings, pinned alternatives and dietary settings. Current recipes, saved-recipe reevaluation and shopping reuse it. Keep optional ingredients outside required totals unless already selected as required. Yes removes additional need regardless of optional quantity/date; explicit No retains need and precedence. Do not overwrite inventory or saved recipes during repricing.

Use observed → existing exact-unit static → unpriced for each uncovered required requirement. Preserve all existing static values. Show per-row basis, applicable source/store/dates and package-versus-allocated amounts. Mixed totals disclose **Includes static estimates**; unknown requirements disclose **Incomplete estimate**. A complete static estimate is still not an observed total. Add no regular-price comparison, discount, stock-up signal or measured pantry savings from observed staple costs.

## 6. Provider rights and bounded operations

[Kroger's terms](https://developer.kroger.com/terms), inspected November 9, contain restrictions on content copies/cache duration (5(e)), comparative-analysis use cases (4(a)(10)), attribution (6(b)) and post-termination stored content (8(b)). The site's software license is not proof of API-content rights. This packet records the relevant constraints; it does not decide the legal applicability of the app's existing cross-store optimizer or grant permission.

One new header-only Production check returned HTTP 200 with no Cache-Control, Expires or Age header (November 9, 23:03:28 UTC); the body was not retained. That establishes no positive cache lifetime. Propose request-only processing and no new raw response/price fixtures in Git until permitted use/retention is documented. On an explicitly permitted future cache, TTL must be no longer than the shortest provider permission/header lifetime, source expiry and same-day cutoff; no-store/private restrictions take precedence. An offline saved Kroger payload cannot bypass this. USDA snapshot retention is a separate source contract.

The earlier checked-in live evidence snapshots also require a retention review. Do not grow that dataset while the gate is unresolved. Propose replacing unnecessary tracked provider payloads with permission-appropriate references/hashes and synthetic test cases after a disposition is agreed; changing working-tree files alone does not purge Git history. No historical rewrite, deletion of user files, provider contact, new terms acceptance or credential change is included here.

Proposed launch bounds: server-only requests; 8-second timeout; at most two concurrent provider calls; at most one retry for network/5xx failures with jitter, respecting provider Retry-After without waiting longer than the request budget. No retry for 401/403; stop on quota exhaustion. A failed refresh does not become “not found” or rewrite prices as zero. Bound response bodies to 2 MiB and returned items to the reviewed detail-response schema. Cancellation invalidates dependent results.

Before public activation, enforce a shared atomic feature budget of at most 1,000 Products calls per rolling 24 hours, counting failures and retries, within the provider allowance and alongside existing connector usage. Limit feature requests to 30/minute per verified account or anonymous client plus shared IP enforcement. Accept only reviewed ingredient/market/location inputs; do not expose a general credential-funded provider proxy. Fetch only selected recipe needs, not all candidates at all four stores on every page view. Use source-permitted coalescing only when rights permit it. Quota/rate-limit failures use static/unpriced fallback. No paid service or distributed limiter implementation is provisioned by this packet; its shared enforcement is an activation check.

Keep lookup/camera and observed-pricing controls independent. Switching off observed pricing restores static/unpriced calculation without rewriting stored requirements, inventory, source history or saved recipes. Keyless CI/manual pantry operation remains deterministic. No user scan history, private ingredient name, frame, account token or credential may enter shared quote storage/logs. Keep only permitted minimized diagnostics with bounded growth.
