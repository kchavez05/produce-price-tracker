# v1.6.2 staple pricing coverage audit

Date: November 9, 2026. Baseline: `8a7e244ca2a3a1dcbc16dae6d1d67a3ca6593afe` (v1.6.1). Status: source and recipe feasibility audited; live launch coverage is **not verified**. This packet approves no shared mapping, conversion, source integration, or release.

## Decisions confirmed in chat

- Use the already downloaded USDA branded snapshot as the product-discovery starting point; it supplies no retail prices.
- Initial observed staple prices: King Soopers in Mead/Firestone/Longmont, applicable to the selected location.
- Only unconditional shelf prices observed on the same America/Denver calendar day qualify initially. Exclude membership, digital coupon and multi-buy conditions; earlier expiry also disqualifies an offer. Static/unpriced fallback remains required.
- Launch camera targets: iPhone Safari and Android Chrome, with real-device verification still pending.

## Reproducible evidence and limits

The [machine-readable packet](v1.6.2-staple-coverage-audit.json) retains all requirements (including recipe/slot/alternative and quantity), static prices, discovery regexes/counts, compact USDA examples, source SHA-256 fingerprints, and reviewed example dispositions. The raw USDA dump remains outside Git and outside the application bundle.

The enumeration loaded the exported `RECIPE_STAPLES` through `node --import tsx` and joined IDs to `cooking-catalog.json`. The USDA pass streamed `food.csv`, selected branded descriptions with the recorded case-insensitive regexes, and joined `branded_food.csv` by string `fdc_id`. Counts include revisions and false positives. Examples were restricted to US market (`United States` or `US`), no supplied discontinued date, and valid GTIN-8/12/13/14 check digits; these filters do not establish currency, retail availability, or launch symbology support. At most two examples per queried identity were retained, ranked by Kroger owner/brand mention, package-weight presence, publication date, then lexicographic FDC ID (all descending). Ranking is discovery sampling only, not runtime revision selection. Both CSV fingerprints were rechecked after reading.

Only the 17 potential direct-basis identities were searched for product examples. The other 26 were enumerated for recipe/static coverage, not searched for USDA product coverage. Discovery sampling is not exhaustive product qualification and contains intentionally retained negative examples. It cannot justify absence claims, approved mapping counts, or store coverage percentages. Ingredient lists are context for reviewing the product as a whole, never evidence that its individual components are owned.

## Integration findings

1. `fetchKrogerLiveOffers` queries five canonical protein/produce/dairy anchors and no staple IDs. Adding terms alone cannot establish correct staple prices.
2. The connector writes `activePrice` directly into `normalizedPricePerUnit`, while `saleUnit` may contain an entire package-size string. Preserve original package price and net amount separately; parse and validate units before allocation. Do not use this existing normalized field as trusted staple evidence.
3. The connector prefers promo price and uses `SHELF_OR_MEMBER`, which cannot prove the approved unconditional policy. Staple admission must establish applicable unconditional shelf price from actual source semantics. Do not substitute a typical regular-price benchmark when observed shelf evidence is missing.
4. `StoreOffer` lacks a structured net amount/basis, explicit currency, stable location-ID field and detailed purchase-condition evidence. Design a staple quote contract or adapter rather than passing partially qualified existing offers straight through.
5. Static costing is duplicated across `evaluatePantryRequirementsByIds`, `pantryShoppingNeeds`, and `applyPantryToRecipe`; the replacement evaluator must feed all current/saved recipe and shopping paths consistently.
6. The curry method explicitly calls for full-fat coconut milk, while discovery returned lite examples. A generic coconut-milk match is insufficient for this recipe. The salmon text calls for extra-virgin olive oil; preserve that scoped requirement when selecting offers.
7. The bean requirements use nominal can ounces (30 or 15 oz), and methods do not specify drained weight. Review and explicitly document gross net-content versus edible/drained basis before admitting canned-bean price calculations; import no drained yield.
8. Grain/flour/spice/garlic/ginger volume or count uses retain existing static estimates. This audit introduces no density, clove-weight, leaf-count, drained-yield, or concentrate-dilution rule. Mustard and tomato paste measured by tablespoon also remain static when sold by mass.

## Live coverage completion checklist

For locations `62000116` (Firestone), `62000080` (Pace), `62000042` (North Main), and `62000102` (Hover), verify configured access and inspect actual current responses using the supported product API and its current usage terms. Record each selected store separately; do not infer stock or price in one location from another. A failed or unavailable location remains an explicit gap.

- Record each actual product/offer ID, market/location, currency, package amount and measurement basis, shelf price and conditions, observation time, expiry, and source revision. A USDA brand match is not a Kroger store listing or price.
- Review exact ingredient/form admission, including negative fixtures (spray, dry beans, marinade, concentrate, lite coconut milk, gluten-free ambiguity, wrong market, unknown units). Retain missing/rejected cases as well as successes.
- Establish a deterministic eligible-offer selection/tie policy without changing pinned foods; deduplicate source revisions before selection. No policy is approved by this discovery ranking.
- Verify same-day America/Denver freshness, selected-location/store filtering, positive finite package denominators, money precision and observed/static/unpriced presentation. Choose and document the cooking-volume convention before implementing cross-unit calculations; customary and nutrition-label approximations must not be mixed silently.
- Calculate per-recipe coverage using the actual selected requirements and offers, including dietary/pinned alternatives and serving changes. Record explicitly static/unpriced gaps. Review whether coverage is meaningful before declaring the pricing release gate passed.
- Capture live contract evidence separately from deterministic CI fixtures. No runtime ingestion, mapping publication, hosted migration or inventory write was performed in this audit.
