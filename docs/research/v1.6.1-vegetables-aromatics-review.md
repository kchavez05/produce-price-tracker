## Review boundaries

- **Leeks:** the source includes bulb and greens with root removed. Review this as the fresh leek identity; no weight conversion, onion ownership, or part-specific recipe coverage is implied.
- **Beets and radishes:** beetroot is an alias for the root identity. Neither choice includes leafy greens or a named radish variety such as daikon.
- **Cucumber:** the selected source includes peel. Peel treatment remains evidence context rather than a second default pantry choice or a portion-conversion claim.
- **Fresh herbs:** fresh cilantro/fresh coriander means leaves, never coriander seed or dried leaf. Parsley and basil remain distinct fresh identities. Fresh spearmint does not establish peppermint or generic-mint recipe coverage.
- **Preservation:** fresh, frozen, canned, dried, and powdered forms remain separate. Search aliases never create coverage; inventory remains explicit Yes/No.
- **Market applicability:** none of the ten new identities has a current counterpart in the 35-item market catalog. They remain explicitly unpriced. No broad vegetable market category is assigned as a substitute.

Broccoli/cauliflower, spinach/kale, white/cremini mushrooms, and tomatoes/tomatillos remain deferred for grouped-market/recipe review. Carrots also remain outside this batch so their applicable market link can be reviewed explicitly with recipe behavior. This package does not resolve or clear any of the 74 held Foundation rows.
